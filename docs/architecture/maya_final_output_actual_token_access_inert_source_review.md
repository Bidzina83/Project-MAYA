Approved source-only contract and fixtures completed 2026-11-10. See
`maya_final_output_actual_token_access_inert_contract.md` for exact masks,
paths, status fields and admission requirements. No role launch or native
open was added. The fixture is excluded without `MAYA_JOURNAL_TOKEN_ACCESS_FIXTURE`.

The runner consumes one bound command and emits only the ordered fixed
control/target matrix. Inputs cannot provide a path, role change, access mask
or alternate command. The admitted reply binds the trusted original process
and pipe objects, role, PID, attempt and request, and requires supplied actual
token, closed writer/noninheritance, EOF/natural-exit and unchanged target
evidence. Those observations are injected, not made by the fixture. The
admission owner consumes its allowance even if evidence is invalid.

Verified denial requires exact matching successful/closed control and access
denied at the target. Missing object, sharing violation, mismatched control,
failed control or close are incomplete. A well-formed forbidden open is a
failure even without a passing control. A malformed reply is rejected, never
treated as denial. The fixture returns per-right coverage and no qualification.
